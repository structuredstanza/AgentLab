Owned OAuth implementation
==========================

The provider OAuth implementations and shared flow behavior in src/oauth/ are
ported/adapted from @oh-my-pi/pi-ai 18.13.3 and @oh-my-pi/pi-catalog 18.13.3,
upstream https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi, version 18.13.3.

Sources include the provider auth rules under pi-catalog/src/compat/rules/auth/,
pi-ai/src/registry/engine/, pi-ai/src/registry/oauth/anthropic.ts,
openai-codex.ts, google-antigravity.ts, their shared PKCE/callback utilities,
and provider identity/header constants. The owned implementation does not call
the upstream OAuth engine or AuthStorage. The separately pinned SDK remains in
use for inference and the model catalog and retains its own license notices.
